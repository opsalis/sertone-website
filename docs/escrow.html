Paying with Your Escrow Balance

What it is

Your wrapper keeps a small prepaid balance (the escrow) in a public payment contract. Most payments to services — AI, apps, API calls, subscriptions — are taken from it instantly, without a blockchain transaction for each payment.

How a payment works

  1. Your wrapper checks that your escrow balance covers the payment (it never lets you spend more than you have).
  2. If it does not, it tops the balance up first from your wallet (one transaction), up to your chosen reserve (default 10 USDC; daily automatic limit default 50 USDC — both changeable in the console and btw.yaml).
  3. It sends the service a signed promise to pay (a voucher). The service collects it on the blockchain, together with many others, in one transaction.

Fees

Every payment carries a 0.8% contract-owner fee, fixed in the contract, and the developer fee named by the software you use (0.2% in this software). There is no other fee on payments.

Transfers between people

Below 100 USD: a plain token transfer, free. From 100 USD: through the escrow, with the fees above. Maximum 5,000 USD per transfer.

If a service pauses you

If a service could not collect what you owe (for example your balance ran out), it keeps your promise and pauses your access until it is paid; your console shows the amount due. As soon as your balance is topped up, your wrapper tells the service, it collects, and your access resumes automatically. A promise to pay never expires until it is paid.

Taking your balance back

Withdrawing your escrow balance takes about one hour: your wrapper first asks every service you owe to collect, then the contract waits one hour so services can do so, then the rest returns to your wallet.

For services (merchants)

Keep your wrapper running 24/7. Collect small payments in batches (default: when worth at least 50× the collection cost, and at least once a day); amounts above 1 USDC are collected before you deliver. If a customer withdraws while your wrapper is offline for more than an hour, small amounts not yet collected are lost; your console warns you when this happens. Earnings go straight to your payout address (your wallet by default).
